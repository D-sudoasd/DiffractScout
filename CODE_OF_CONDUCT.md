# Code of conduct

Project participants must communicate professionally and respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate misrepresentation of scientific evidence, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Technical disagreement should be resolved through reproducible examples, cited definitions, test cases, and documented assumptions. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive material and may restrict participation after repeated or severe violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to `dlgong17s@imr.ac.cn`. Reports should include the relevant links, dates, and a factual description. The maintainer will limit disclosure to people needed to assess and resolve the report.
